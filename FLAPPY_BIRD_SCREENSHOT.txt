================================================================================
                           GAMEPLAY VISUALIZATION
================================================================================

PHYSICS:
  Gravity:    ↓ 980 px/s²
  Jump:       ↑ -350 px/s
  Scroll:     ← 200 px/s (pipes/ground)
              ← 50 px/s (background - parallax)

COLLISION ZONES:
  Bird: 40x40 box (20px radius)
  Pipes: 80px wide body + 100px wide cap
  Ground: y > 600
  Ceiling: y < 20

GAME FLOW:
  1. Press SPACE → Bird jumps upward
  2. Gravity pulls bird down continuously
  3. Navigate through pipe gaps
  4. Score +1 for each pipe passed
  5. Hit pipe/ground/ceiling → Game Over
  6. Press R → Restart game
